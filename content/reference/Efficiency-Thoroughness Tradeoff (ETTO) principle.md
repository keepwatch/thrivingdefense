# Efficiency-Thoroughness Tradeoff (ETTO) principle

Per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Efficiency%E2%80%93thoroughness_trade-off_principle):
> there is a trade-off between efficiency or effectiveness on one hand, and thoroughness ... on the other

Said another way:
> Maximizing efficiency without a constraint wastes resources via opportunity cost

The term was coined by Erik Hollnagel in a 2009 book, *The ETTO principle: efficiency-thoroughness trade-off: why things that go right sometimes go wrong*.
