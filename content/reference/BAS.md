BAS = Breach & Attack Simulation

This is a product class designed to test detection and prevention controls against known attacks. BAS tools are one approach for [[Validating vendor detection effectiveness]], though they are limited to the test scenarios provided by the vendor. For [[detection libraries must be repeatedly validated|ongoing validation]], scheduled BAS runs can be used to catch regressions over time.
