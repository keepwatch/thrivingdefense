Deep research into techniques — like what's documented in [[Technique Research Report (TRR)]]s — enables a comprehensive understanding of a given technique. That means each procedure within the technique can have a validation test written for it, and that test can be run once or [[detection libraries must be repeatedly validated|repeatedly]]. In this way, any organization can conclusively prove they have coverage for the [single procedure possible for DCSync](https://github.com/tired-labs/techniques/blob/main/reports/trr0011/ad/README.md#procedure-a-request-replication-from-a-dc), for example.
