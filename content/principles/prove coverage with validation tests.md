Deep research into techniques (like that documented in [[Technique Research Report (TRR)]]s) allows comprehensive understanding of a given technique. This means each procedure within the technique can have a validation test written for it, and that test could be executed once or [[detection libraries must be repeatedly validated|repeatedly]]. In this manner, any organization can conclusively prove that they have coverage for the [single procedure possible for DCSync](https://github.com/tired-labs/techniques/blob/main/reports/trr0011/ad/README.md#procedure-a-request-replication-from-a-dc), for example.
