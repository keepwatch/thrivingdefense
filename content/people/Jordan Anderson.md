Jordan has been in cybersecurity for 13 years, working in multiple roles including triage, SIEM professional services, incident response, and detection engineering. He currently leads multiple functions within Bloomberg's Cyber Security Operations Center (CSOC), notably Detection Engineering and Technology Oversight. 

Jordan is eager to work with other practitioners to turn the art of cybersecurity into a science, with clear understanding of what works and why, especially for the relatively young field of detection engineering.
